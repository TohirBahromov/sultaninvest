# Surface brief: sultaninvest.uz marketing site (all routes)

Scope: Home, Services (+ one page per service), Work, About, Contact, in uz (/), ru (/ru), en (/en). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: SMB owners and personal brands in Uzbekistan, often arriving on a phone from Instagram/Telegram. Job: decide this crew is capable, then leave a phone number or message on Telegram.
Proof on hand: real crew names and roles, Sultan Edu (sultanedu.uz) as a real web case. Stats are approximate placeholders. No showreel, case media, client logos or testimonials yet: never invent them.
Constraints: brand colors #051A07 / #E9DBBC / #1D1D1D (+ #322821, #D4A276) and SQI logo are fixed; static export; light on phones; lead form posts to /api/lead.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every visit plays as a film's opening titles: the agency is a film crew, the services are the credits, and the visitor's project is the next production. Refuses the category default of a headline-over-video hero followed by a row of equal service cards and a logo strip.

OWN-WORLD: Deep forest "screen" (#051A07) framed by true-black letterbox bars (#0A0A08 off the charcoal), alternating with champagne (#E9DBBC) intertitle cards set in forest ink, so the page breathes between projection and printed card. Display in a high-contrast didone (Noto Serif Display) echoing the SQI monogram; credits, labels and UI in light, widely tracked geometric caps (Jost) like the logo's subline. Timecodes, scene/take numbers and the clapperboard slate are the system's labels and its placeholder language. Hairline champagne rules, film grain, no rounded cards, no gradients-as-decoration.

STORY: The visitor sees in one viewport that this is a production house that also does SMM and web, believes it because the whole crew is credited by name for every discipline, and leaves a phone number in the bar under the screen or opens Telegram.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed black. Top letterbox bar: SQI monogram left, five tracked-caps links, uz/ru/en, phone. Center: a 2.39:1 screen, edge to edge, running a typographic title sequence ("Sultan Quick Invest presents" → headline in didone at ~9vw: "Video, SMM and web. One crew.") over grain, with the showreel MP4 behind it when supplied. Bottom letterbox bar: a credit line of the four disciplines left; right, the working action: +998 phone field and "Call me back" button, Telegram link beside it. Running timecode in the bar's corner.

FORM: Opening Titles, candidate 1 on the ordered list (pick card, user-chosen over assigned candidate 6); seed key f1fbccb6.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The crew credit roll: a pinned screen where disciplines and the real crew names roll upward like end credits as you scroll, tying every service to named people (the "one crew" proof). Motion grammar: fades and slow vertical credit rolls, letterbox bars that open and close between scenes, scene-number counters; parallax only on framed stills. Reduced motion shows everything static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
